About the Project:

SolarWise Algeria is an AI-assisted solar energy platform concept designed to help Algerian households, businesses, farmers, and residents of remote villages explore solar energy solutions.
Many people, particularly those in rural and isolated communities, may face difficulties understanding solar system requirements, comparing costs, and finding solar equipment or qualified installers. 
SolarWise Algeria aims to make this information more accessible and support informed decisions about renewable energy.

Key Features:
The prototype is designed to bring several solar-energy services together

Solar assessment: Explore energy needs and potential solar system requirements.
Cost comparison: Compare conventional electricity costs with estimated solar costs and potential savings.
Equipment marketplace: Explore solar panels, batteries, inverters, and related equipment.
Solar service directory: Help users discover solar specialists and installation services.
Local relevance: Focus on the needs of Algerian households, farms, businesses, and remote communities.

Who Is It For?

Households interested in solar energy.
Farmers and agricultural businesses.
Small and medium-sized businesses.
Residents of rural villages and remote areas.
People exploring solar equipment and installation services in Algeria.

Future Roadmap:
Over the next three months, planned improvements include

1- Validate solar estimates and financial calculations using reliable data.
2- Improve accessibility and usability for rural and remote communities.
3- Explore integration of reliable solar-resource data.
4- Develop secure user accounts and persistent data storage.
5- Improve equipment listings and installer verification.
6- Test the platform with potential users and incorporate their feedback.
